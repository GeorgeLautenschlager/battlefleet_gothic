# 02 — The Turn (pp. 50–51)

## Overview (p. 50)

- Players **alternate turns**. In their turn, a player moves and fights with **all** their ships: first all movement, then all shooting, etc.
- A **game turn** = both players' turns.

### Who gets the first turn
- Normally both players roll a D6; the highest scorer chooses to go first or second.
- Some scenarios dictate this (e.g. in an ambush, the attacker always goes first).

### Ending the battle
- Commonly: fight until one side surrenders or has no ships left on the table.
- Some scenarios allow a "sudden death" victory (e.g. destroying the enemy flagship).
- Or end at a preset time / turn limit. See [11-scenarios.md](11-scenarios.md).

### Exceptions
- Some actions happen outside your own turn (e.g. **Brace For Impact!**). Events may interrupt a turn; after the interruption, the turn sequence continues normally.

## Turn Sequence (p. 51)

Each player turn has four phases, in order:

### 1. Movement Phase
- First, **remove any special order markers** you placed last turn — **except** ships on **Brace For Impact!** (they keep it this turn).
- Then move ships and squadrons **one at a time**. Before moving a ship/squadron, you may take a **Command check** to put it on special orders. **If any Command check fails, no further checks may be made this turn.**
- Ships must always move **at least half their speed** unless using **Burn Retros**.
- See [03-movement.md](03-movement.md).

### 2. Shooting Phase
- The player shoots with any of their ships within range of enemy vessels (direct fire), and launches ordnance.
- See [04-shooting.md](04-shooting.md) and [06-ordnance.md](06-ordnance.md).

### 3. Ordnance Phase
- **Both players** may move any Ordnance markers (torpedoes, attack craft) still on the table that were launched in a previous turn.
- See [06-ordnance.md](06-ordnance.md).

### 4. End Phase
- **Both players** may attempt to repair critical damage on their ships.
- **D6 Blast Markers are removed** by the player whose turn it is.
- See [07-end-phase.md](07-end-phase.md).
