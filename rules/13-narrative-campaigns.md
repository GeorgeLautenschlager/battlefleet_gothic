# 13 — Narrative Campaigns: Third Armageddon War & 13th Black Crusade (pp. 161–199)

The narrative/lore pages (pp. 162–176 and 182–189) are background fiction with no game rules and are **not transcribed**. Only the scenarios are captured here. Sub-sector maps (Gothic Sector p. 161, Armageddon Sector p. 177, 13th Black Crusade p. 190) are images.

**Sub-sector map key** (pp. 161, 177, 190): **A** Agri-world, **C** Civilised world, **F** Forge world, **H** Hive world, **M** Mining world, **P** Penal world, **U** Uninhabited system.

Ship profiles for named classes (Planet Killer, Mars, Tyrant, Gothic, Cobra, Iconoclast, Ork ships, etc.) are **not in this rulebook** — they are in the separate *Remastered Fleets* book.

## Third Armageddon War (941.M41)

### Scenario One: The Gauntlet (p. 178)
- **Forces**: Imperial convoy of **≥ 2 transports**; for every two transports, the Imperial **and** Ork players may each take **100 pts** of ships. Heavy Transport rules may be used. Imperial: only **one Cruiser**; all others Light Cruisers or Escorts. Orks: **Escorts only**.
- **Battlezone**: Primary Biosphere generator; ignore planet results.
- **Set-up**: Imperial convoy and escorts within **45 cm of one short edge**, facing the opposite edge. Orks move on from any point on either long edge in their first turn.
- **First turn**: Ork player.
- **Game length**: until one fleet is destroyed or disengages, or the Imperial fleet exits from the far short edge.
- **Victory**: Imperial must exit **≥ 3 transports** off the opposite short edge; otherwise Ork victory.
- **Optional "Running Battles" variant**: Imperial ships start within 30 cm of the table centre facing either short edge. At the end of every Imperial turn, every ship and phenomenon is moved back 20 cm away from the edge the Imperial ships originally faced; anything dropping off the table has disengaged. At the end of each Imperial turn roll a D6: on a 6 the Imperial player places a random phenomenon (no planets) on the edge his ships faced. Game lasts **10 turns**; Imperial wins with ≥ 3 transports still on the table.

### Scenario Two: Parol's Bait (p. 179)
A variant of *The Bait* ([11-scenarios.md](11-scenarios.md#scenario-two-the-bait-p-130)).
- **Forces**: Ork pursuers up to **500 pts**. Imperial pursued up to **250 pts** initially (Light Cruisers and Escorts only; more than one ship/squadron may start) plus up to **500 pts** of reinforcements, from which up to **six Orbital Mines** may be bought (they start on the table).
- **Battlezone**: D6 1–4 outer reaches, 5–6 primary biosphere.
- **Set-up**: pursued ships in table centre facing a short edge; mines anywhere in front of them. Orks ≥ 60 cm behind. Imperial reinforcements enter from the short edge the pursued ships face.
- **First turn**: Imperial.
- **Special**: reinforcements may enter any turn; after turn 1, up to 30 cm along the long edges per turn after the first.
- **Game length**: until one fleet is destroyed or disengages.
- **Victory**: standard VPs; Orks also gain **half the points value of any Imperial reinforcements** brought on. Mines' VPs automatically go to the attacker.

### Scenario Three: Pelucidar (pp. 180–181)
- **Forces**: Imperial up to **1,500 pts** — Cruisers, Battlecruisers, Battleships only (historically Armageddon-class battleships); up to **four Minefields**. Orks start with **no ships** and receive them randomly.
- **Battlezone**: Outer Reaches. After random phenomena, the Imperial player places the planet **Pelucidar** anywhere, then deploys his entire fleet. (The text refers to a deployment map that does not appear in v1.10; the Set-up text repeats The Raiders': all ships facing the same table edge, ≥ 30 cm from any edge, ships/squadrons ≥ 20 cm apart.) Orks move on from any point along their table edge.
- **First turn**: Orks.
- **Special**: at the start of each Ork turn, roll **D6 + turn number**:

| D6 + Turn | Ork arrivals |
|---|---|
| 2–4 | 1 Fighta-Bomma / Attack Craft Wing (4 counters) |
| 5–6 | 1 Escort Squadron (D6 escorts of any type) |
| 7–9 | 2 Escort Squadrons |
| 10 | 3 Escort Squadrons |
| 11 | 1 Kroozer or Terror Ship |
| 12 | 2 Kroozers or Terror Ships |
| 13–14 | 1 Rok |
| 15+ | 1 Space Hulk |

  If you lack the models, take the next lowest result.
- **Game length**: until the Imperial fleet is destroyed or has disengaged.
- **Victory**: Imperial scores VPs for Ork ships destroyed/crippled (not for holding the field), **minus** the VPs of any Imperial ship destroyed **or disengaged**. Orks score none. Imperial needs **≥ 2,000 VPs** to win; otherwise Orks win.

## Abaddon's 13th Black Crusade (999.M41)

All scenarios use a **180 × 120 cm** table.

### Scenario One: Chance Encounter (p. 191)
- **Forces (fixed)**: Chaos — Planet Killer (Warmaster Ld 9, 3 re-rolls), Devastation cruiser, Slaughter cruiser, squadron of three Iconoclast destroyers. Imperial — Mars battlecruiser (Admiral Ld 9, 2 re-rolls), Tyrant cruiser, Gothic cruiser, squadron of four Cobra destroyers.
- **Set-up**: Chaos places the Planet Killer on the centre line of its long edge, 10 cm in. Players alternate placing a cruiser or escort squadron within their zone (up to **30 cm** from their long edge). Map: Chaos zone is a **60 cm**-wide block centred on the top edge; Imperial zone spans the bottom edge; **60 cm** between zones.
- **First turn**: Imperial chooses.
- **Game length**: 6 turns.
- **Victory**: Planet Killer destroyed → Imperial major victory; crippled → Imperial minor victory. If the Planet Killer and one other Chaos capital ship exit off the opposite edge → Chaos victory (unless the Planet Killer is crippled).

### Scenario Two: Rearguard Attack (p. 192)
- **Forces**: agreed limit. Forces of Disorder (defender) up to the limit, with **25%** split off as reinforcements. Forces of Order (attacker) up to **half**.
- **Set-up**: defender deploys all, facing the **sunward** edge, ≥ 30 cm from any long edge and ≥ 60 cm from the sunward edge; ships/squadrons ≥ 10 cm apart. Attacker moves on from any edge in his first turn.
- **First turn**: attacker.
- **Special**: **Ambushed** — defender −1 Ld for the first D6 turns. **Reinforcements** enter from the sunward edge on any turn; after turn 1, up to 30 cm along the long edges per turn after the first.
- **Game length**: 8 turns or until one fleet disengages. **Victory**: standard VPs.

### Scenario Three: Alien Intervention (p. 193)
- **Forces**: Forces of Disorder (attacker) up to the agreed limit. Forces of Order (defender) up to **75%**, plus **25%** chosen from the **Eldar** list as reinforcements.
- **Battlezone**: must include **one planet** and a **drifting hulk** within 30 cm of the table centre.
- **Set-up**: defender within 30 cm of the table centre line, ships/squadrons ≥ 10 cm apart, all facing the same way. Attacker within 20 cm of either short edge.
- **First turn**: attacker rolls 2D6, defender rolls D6; high roll chooses.
- **Special**: Eldar arrive (whole fleet, from one long edge) on a D6 at the start of the Forces of Order's turn: turn 2 on **4+**, turn 3 **3+**, turn 4 **2+**, turn 5 **2+**, turn 6 automatic.
- **Game length**: 8 turns or until only one fleet remains. **Victory**: standard VPs.

### Scenario Four: Above Belis Corona (pp. 194–195)
- **Forces**: Attacker (Disorder) — **1,500 pts** split into two forces as equal as possible. Defender (Order) — **750 pts** (no battleships) + one free re-roll, plus **2 space stations, 1 orbital dock, 8 orbital mines, 2 system ships, 1 defence monitor**, and **8 platforms** (any mix of orbital defence laser platforms, orbital torpedo launchers, orbital weapons platforms) split into 4 **outer** and 4 **inner**.
- **Battlezone**: planet Belis Corona dead centre, gravity well **30 cm**. Each player places an asteroid field ≥ 30 cm from the planet.
- **Set-up**: (1) defender places dock and stations in the gravity well, and 4 outer platforms anywhere. (2) Attacker places half his fleet within 10 cm of one short edge and half within 10 cm of the other. (3) Defender places inner platforms, system ships, monitor and mines within 30 cm of the planet. (4) Remaining defending ships are in reserve.
- **First turn**: attacker 2D6 vs defender D6; high roll chooses.
- **Special**: each reserve ship/squadron rolls D6 at the start of the defender's turn: speed ≤ 20 cm **5+**, 25 cm **4+**, 30 cm+ **3+** (slowest ship in a mixed squadron). After failing twice, +1 cumulative per turn. Enter from the designated long edge.
- **Game length**: 8 turns.
- **Victory**: attacker wins only by destroying **both space stations, the orbital dock, all four inner platforms, and 25% (points) of the defender's reinforcement fleet**. Otherwise defender wins.

### Scenario Five: Daemon Blockade (p. 196)
A variant of *Blockade Run*.
- **Forces**: Chaos blockader spends the agreed total **minus 100**, plus **ten Daemon Mines**. Forces of Order (attacker) spend up to half.
- **Battlezone**: outer reaches or deep space.
- **Set-up**: table split lengthways into thirds; D6 per blockading ship/squadron/Daemon mine for its third; any facing; none within 60 cm of the attacker's edge. Attacker within 15 cm of his edge.
- **First turn**: roll-off; highest decides.
- **Daemon Mines**: as orbital mines except: move **10+D6 cm** each Ordnance Phase; devour any enemy ordnance contacted (incl. torpedoes) with no effect on themselves — except **fighters**: on contact roll D6, mine survives on 4+; the fighter is removed regardless.
- **Game length**: 6 turns.
- **Victory**: standard VPs + attacker scores the points of ships moved off via the blockader's edge (crippled = ¼). Destroyed (not detonated) Daemon Mines are worth **10 pts** each to the attacker.

### Scenario Six: Navigate the Storm (p. 197)
- **Forces**: Chaos **750 pts**. Imperial **1,000 pts + 10 transports**.
- **Battlezone**: each player places **eight Warp Rifts** alternately, each ≥ 10 cm from others.
- **Set-up**: both deploy secretly (screen or sketch map), then reveal. Map (approximate): Chaos zone along the top edge toward the right; the **Exit Point** is a strip on the right short edge; Imperial zone toward the left (a 60 cm strip on the left edge, around 90 cm / 20 cm markers); "Attacker's Deployment" labelled along the bottom edge.
- **First turn**: Chaos chooses.
- **Special**: **Transformation** — a Chaos ship that fails to navigate a Warp Rift becomes a **Daemonship** (permanent) and redeploys via Warp Translation in the current Chaos End Phase (repeatable).
- **Game length**: until victory conditions are met or no transports remain.
- **Victory**: Chaos needs to destroy **8 transports** AND cripple/destroy **50%** (points) of the Imperial fleet (ships lost in Warp Rifts count). Imperial needs to exit **5 transports** via the Exit Point AND move **25%** (points, uncrippled) of his fleet off the same edge. Otherwise, whoever has more destroyed (Chaos) / escaped (Imperial) transports claims a minor victory.

### Scenario Seven: Macharia's End (pp. 198–199)
- **Forces**: Chaos **1,500 pts + the Planet Killer**. Imperial **2,000 pts**, up to 500 of which may form a **Vanguard**.
- **Battlezone**: planet **Macharia** at the centre of the Imperial table edge.
- **Set-up**: Imperial main fleet within 20 cm of his edge; Chaos fleet (minus Planet Killer) within 10 cm of his edge; Imperial vanguard within 5 cm of the centre line; the Planet Killer moves on from the Chaos edge in turn 1.
- **First turn**: Imperial.
- **Special — Armageddon Overcharge**: at the start of a Chaos turn the player may begin charging (place a counter, and another at the start of each following turn). At **3 counters** the gun must fire that Shooting Phase. Once started, charging can't stop. **While charging**: the Planet Killer can't turn, take special orders or fire lances, and gains **+2 shields**. **Firing**: place the Nova Cannon template touching its stem and move it **directly ahead 60 cm**; any ship whose base the template passes over is **obliterated**; if the template hole touches Macharia it is destroyed and the game ends. **Recharging** after a miss: must pass **two Reload Ordnance orders in consecutive turns**; meanwhile it can only move.
- **Game length**: until Macharia is destroyed or the Planet Killer is crippled.
- **Victory**: Chaos must destroy Macharia; Imperial must cripple the Planet Killer.
