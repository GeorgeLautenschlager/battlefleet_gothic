# 12 — Campaign Rules (pp. 144–160)

A map-based system linking battles together. Players fight over systems in a sub-sector, earn **renown**, and their ships gain experience. The commander with the most renown at the deadline wins.

## Getting Started (p. 146)
- Needs at least one Chaos and one Imperial player; Ork/Eldar players can join.
- Pick a sub-sector map (smaller ones for first campaigns). Track system control.
- Decide campaign length (first campaign: **one month of real time** recommended). Optionally elect an arbitrator.

## Starting Systems (p. 147)
- Most systems start **neutral**.
- Imperial and Chaos players each start controlling **one system** (roll D6 for picking order).
- Ork and Eldar players have a secret **pirate base** in a system instead (may be in an Imperial/Chaos-controlled system).

## Starting Fleets & Fleet Register (p. 147)
- **2,000 pts** for Imperial and Chaos; **1,500 pts** for Ork and Eldar. Must have models for all ships.
- Each player keeps a **fleet register** (commissioning, class, fleet trials, history, losses). A vessel used before its "fleet trials" (model not assembled/painted) suffers a critical hit on a D6 roll of 1 each time it uses special orders.
- Ships added after the campaign start have **−1 Leadership**.
- Battle forces must be picked from the register.

## The Commander (p. 147)
- Starts with **1 renown** and **one re-roll**. Choose a **flagship**; the commander's re-roll/abilities only work if the flagship is in the battle.
- If the flagship is destroyed the commander escapes but has no further effect that game; pick a new flagship afterwards.

## Fighting Battles (pp. 147–148)
- Challenge other players. Recommended: Imperial players don't fight each other (split into loyalists/rebels if needed).
- Optional penalties for inactivity: no game for a fortnight → −1 renown; for a month → a random system rebels to another player.

## The Campaign Turn (p. 148)
Each game gives both players a campaign turn:
1. **Build-Up**: determine initiative; roll incoming orders; select target system; select forces.
2. **Fight battle**.
3. **Aftermath**: spoils of war, adjust renown, promotions/demotions, ship experience, repairs & withdrawals, appeals.

## Build-Up (pp. 149–150)

1. **Determine initiative**: roll-off, highest is attacker. The player with **fewer systems gets +1**. (Optionally use attack ratings.)
2. **Receive orders** — D6:

| D6 | Orders |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Raid (500–750 pts) |
| 3–6 | Battle (750–1,500 pts) |

   A player with **21+ renown** may choose raid or battle (if both, roll to see who decides).
   - **Decide scenario**: roll on Raids/Battles tables (same as [11-scenarios.md](11-scenarios.md#choosing-a-scenario-p-120)) or agree.
   - **Decide location**: the attacker chooses a system connected by a warp route to one they control (any, if none held). Uninhabited system → defender's points reduced by **100**.
   - **Decide size**: agree points within the order's limits. Each extra system a player holds over their opponent **reduces their fleet size by 10 pts**.
   - **Ork & Eldar** only ever raid: a rolled "battle" becomes a **major raid** (roll on Raids table, 750–1,500 pts).
3. **Pick fleets** from the register (no changes to ships).
4. Roll **sub-plots** and fight.

### Attacking Pirate Bases (p. 150)
- An opponent of rank **Admiral or higher** (or equivalent) who gains initiative may hunt the base: roll **D6×10**; if **under the Ork/Eldar player's renown**, the base is found (revealed permanently). Otherwise play a normal scenario.
- If found and in a system the attacker controls or a neutral one, play **Planetary Assault** or **Exterminatus**; if the attacker wins, the base is destroyed.

## Aftermath (pp. 151–156)

### 1. Spoils of War
- An attacker who wins a **battle** may claim the system if it is connected by a warp route to one they control, and it belonged to the defeated player or was neutral.
- An attacker who wins a **raid** counts the system as theirs for the rest of this campaign turn (affects repairs).

### 2. Adjust Renown (p. 152)

| Renown gained for | |
|---|---|
| Winning a battle or major raid | +2 |
| Winning a raid | +1 |
| Victory points earned | +(VPs / 100, round up) |
| Sub-plots | variable |
| Each capital ship hulk captured | +1 |
| Fought commander with higher renown | +1 |
| Fought fleet with higher points value and lost | +1 |
| Fought fleet with higher points value and won | +2 |

| Renown lost for | |
|---|---|
| Losing a battle | −1 |
| Losing a raid | −1 |
| Each capital ship lost | −1 |
| Sub-plots | variable |

Renown can never drop below 1. Renown changes apply even if the commander's flagship wasn't present.

### 4. Ship Experience (p. 154)
- **Gaining experience**: ships that fought and were **not crippled or destroyed** roll **2D6**; if **higher than their Ld**, either +1 Ld (max 10) **or** roll on Crew Skills. Ships with **Ld 6 or 7 must take the +1 Ld**.
- **Crippled ships**: −1 Ld (min 6). Crew skills are not lost.
- **Destroyed ships**: replaced by a new ship (rename): **Ld 6**, no crew skills, refits lost.
- **Escort squadrons**: as ships; ≥ 50% casualties = crippled; wiped out = destroyed.

### 5. Repairs & Withdrawals (p. 155)
- Each controlled system repairs damage points per the table. **All criticals are repaired automatically** (including Bridge Smashed, Shields Collapse). Repair points can replace lost escorts (1 point per escort).

| Renown | Agri | Penal (min. 1) | Mining | Forge, hive, pirate world | Civilised | Uninhabited |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| 6–10 | 1 | D6−4 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 11–20 | 1 | D6−3 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 1 |
| 21–30 | 2 | D6−2 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 1 |
| 31–50 | 2 | D6−1 | 3 | 6 | 5 | 1 |
| 51+ | 3 | D6 | 4 | 12 | 6 | 1 |

- Unrepaired damage carries over into the next battle (the ship starts with fewer hits).
- **Withdrawing**: a ship may be withdrawn for full repair — unavailable for the next game, then returns at full hits (escort squadrons at full strength).

### 6. Appeals (p. 156)

| Renown | Appeals |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | 1 |
| 11–30 | 2 |
| 31–50 | 3 |
| 51+ | 4 |

- Declare all appeals first (the same type at most twice), then roll a D6 each:

| Appeal | Granted on |
|---|---|
| Reinforcements (one capital ship or up to five escorts; must own the models) | 2+ |
| Refits | 4+ |
| Other (allies, e.g. Space Marines, Chaos powers — see fleet lists) | 5+ |

- **Refit**: choose a capital ship, roll D6: **1–2 ship refit**, **3–4 engine refit**, **5–6 weapon refit**, then roll on that table (re-roll duplicates). Ship's points value **+10% per refit**.

## Conclusion (p. 157)
At the deadline, highest renown wins. Alternatives: first to control five systems; play until the sub-sector is entirely Chaos (Chaos + Ork win) or Imperial (Imperial + Eldar win); convoy runs; Ork Waaagh! campaigns.

## General Upgrades (pp. 158–159)

### Engine Refit (D6)
| D6 | Refit |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Secondary Reactors**: extra **2D6** on All Ahead Full. |
| 2 | **Evasive Jets**: at the start of the enemy Shooting Phase, pass a Ld test to make an immediate 45° turn; no special orders next turn. |
| 3 | **Manoeuvring Thrusters**: distance needed before turning reduced by **5 cm**. |
| 4 | **Arrester Engines**: **+1 Ld** for Burn Retros and Come To New Heading. |
| 5 | **Auxiliary Power Relays**: **+5 cm** speed. |
| 6 | **Navigational Shields**: no speed reduction for moving through Blast Markers (incl. gas/dust clouds). |

### Ship Refit (D6)
| D6 | Refit |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Improved Sensor Array**: **+2** (instead of +1) to Command checks for enemy ships on special orders. |
| 2 | **Additional Shield Generator**: **+1 Shields**. |
| 3 | **Superior Damage Control**: one extra die for End Phase repairs. |
| 4 | **Reinforced Hull**: damage **+25%** (round up), speed **−5 cm**. |
| 5 | **Improved Logic Engines**: no −1 Ld for Blast Markers in contact. |
| 6 | **Overload Shield Capacitors**: for each hit on the shields roll D6; on a 6 the hit is ignored and no Blast Marker is placed. |

### Weapons Refit (D6)
| D6 | Refit |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Extra Turrets**: **+1 Turrets**. |
| 2 | **Turbo-weapons**: no right column shift for firing over 30 cm. |
| 3 | **Targeting Matrix**: weapons batteries get a **left column shift** (before other shifts). |
| 4 | **Auto-loaders**: **+1 Ld** for Reload Ordnance (re-roll if the ship has no ordnance). |
| 5 | **Superior Fire Control**: **+1 Ld** for Lock On. |
| 6 | **Motion-Tracking Targeters**: on All Ahead Full, Burn Retros or Come To New Heading, firepower/lance Strength reduced by **25%** (round up) instead of halved. |

### Crew Skills (D6)
| D6 | Skill |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Expert Gunnery**: for Lock On, roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 2 | **Skilled Engineers**: for All Ahead Full or Burn Retros, roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 3 | **Adept Trimsman**: for Come To New Heading, roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 4 | **Excellent Pilots**: bombers re-roll the number of to-hit rolls; this ship's fighters that intercept stay in play on a 4+. Re-roll if no attack craft (Eldar and Space Marines re-roll this skill). |
| 5 | **Disciplined Crew**: once per battle, re-roll a failed Ld test or Command check. |
| 6 | **Elite Command Crew**: once per battle, automatically pass a Ld test or Command check. |

## Sub-Sector Maps (pp. 160–161)
- Only systems of military/strategic importance are shown, linked by **warp channels** (attacks must follow them).
- **System types**: uninhabited, agri-world, mining world, hive world, penal colony, forge world, civilised world. Type affects repairs and orbital defences.
- **Blackstone Fortresses** are marked; optionally include one in that system's defences.
- **Port Maw** system: double planetary defences.
- The maps themselves (p. 161, Gothic Sector) are images and are not transcribed here.
