# 05 — Damage (pp. 65–71)

## Taking Hits (p. 65)

- Record hits taken on the fleet roster.
- When a ship has lost **half its damage points** it is **crippled**.
- When it has lost **all** its hits it is out of action; roll on the **Catastrophic Damage Table** (capital ships).

### Crippled Ships (p. 65)
A ship that has lost half its damage points (e.g. a Lunar with 8 hits is crippled after 4 damage):
- **Halves** shields, turrets, ordnance, **all weapons**, and affects boarding.
- This halving is **cumulative with Brace For Impact!** — braced + crippled = weapons and ordnance halved (rounding up) again.
- **Speed reduced by 5 cm.**
- **Cannot fire its nova cannon.**

## Brace For Impact! — full rules (p. 66)

- May be declared **ANY time** a ship faces taking damage, **before the to-hit roll** (including ramming/being rammed and asteroid fields). It also protects vs critical damage from any kind of **Hit-and-Run** attack.
- Must be decided before the opponent rolls any dice for the attack, including modifier rolls for variable weapons (e.g. Ork Gunz). **Against ordnance, decide before rolling turrets.**
- Does **NOT** protect against critical damage caused by hits that were not saved normally, nor any damage in a boarding action (including critical damage).
- The ship is on Brace For Impact! **until the end of its next turn**, replacing any other special order. However, the effect of the order it replaced remains (e.g. reloaded ordnance stays reloaded; a ship on All Ahead Full must continue it). It still cannot take special orders in its next turn.
- **4+ save on a D6** against actual damage to the ship itself — **NOT** against hits absorbed by shields, reactive armour, holofields etc.
- While braced (until end of its next turn): **no special orders** in its next turn; **Firepower, ordnance and armament Strength are halved** (cumulative with crippled); **Nova Cannon cannot fire**. **Turrets and shields are unaffected.**
- Only works against attacks whose hit rolls are made **after** the order is declared — you can't wait to see how accurate a salvo is.
- If a ship **fails** the check to brace, it cannot try again until the current attacking ship/squadron/ordnance wave/event finishes its attacks; it may try again before the next attacker.

## Critical Hits (pp. 66–67)

- Roll a D6 **for each hit scored on the ship** (not hits absorbed by shields). On **1–5** no extra effect; on a **6** the hit causes **critical damage**: roll **2D6** on the Critical Hits Table.
- Damage caused **by** a critical hit does not itself generate further critical rolls.
- A critical check is made for **every** point of damage on a ship, whatever the source.
- **Escorts that suffer a critical hit for any reason** (including in boarding) are **automatically destroyed**.
- Repairable criticals are **cumulative**: multiple instances of the same critical must all be repaired before the system works again.
- Speed is only reduced once by multiple **Thrusters Damaged** criticals, but all must be repaired to regain normal speed.
- Damage from multiple **Fire!** criticals is cumulative (3 unrepaired fires → 3 damage, and all three keep burning).
- If a critical is rolled that **cannot be applied** (e.g. no dorsal weapons, or a repeat of an unrepairable one like Shields Collapse), apply the **next highest** result instead (e.g. no dorsal armament → Starboard Armament Damaged).

### Critical Hits Table (p. 67)

| 2D6 | Extra damage | Result |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | +0 | **Dorsal Armament Damaged**: dorsal armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 3 | +0 | **Starboard Armament Damaged**: starboard (right) armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Port Armament Damaged**: port (left) armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Prow Armament Damaged**: prow armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 6 | +1 | **Engine Room Damaged**: the ship may not make any turns until repaired. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!**: roll to repair (extinguish) in the End Phase; if not put out it causes 1 point of extra damage and keeps burning. |
| 8 | +1 | **Thrusters Damaged**: reduce speed by 10 cm until repaired. |
| 9 | +0 | **Bridge Smashed**: Leadership reduced by 3. **Cannot be repaired.** If this is the Fleet Commander's flagship, any remaining Fleet Commander re-rolls are lost for the rest of the battle. |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields Collapse**: shield Strength reduced to zero. **Cannot be repaired.** |
| 11 | +D3 | **Hull Breach**: a huge gash torn in the hull. |
| 12 | +D6 | **Bulkhead Collapse**: internal structure collapses. |

"Extra damage" is additional damage points inflicted on the ship by the critical (these do not roll for further criticals).

## Blast Markers (pp. 68–69)

Blast Markers (BMs) represent explosions, debris, radiation, etc. They stay in place until removed in a later End Phase. **They never move once placed.**

### Placing Blast Markers
Place BMs:
- **For each hit absorbed by a ship's shields.**
- For ships reduced to 0 damage, as specified on the Catastrophic Damage Table.
- For each **missed nova cannon** attack.

Placement for shield hits:
- The **first** BM goes in contact with the target's base **directly in the line of fire** from the shooter; subsequent BMs fan out around it. BMs cannot be stacked unless there is physically no more room around the base.
- If the ship under fire has other ships' bases in contact, the BM goes between the target's base and the ship closest in the line of fire. A single round of shooting from a ship/squadron cannot place BMs in contact with more than one other vessel besides the target (two total), except:
  1. A ship with multiple shields taking 3–4 BMs that, when fanned normally, physically touch more than one vessel in actual base contact.
  2. Ships whose bases are actually stacked/overlapping a ship in contact with BMs.
- A ship in base contact with a BM is affected over its **whole base** — it counts as in contact all around it and in every arc for leadership, movement, shooting, ordnance, boarding, etc. This includes if it ended its movement in contact with a BM for any reason.
- With stacked/overlapping bases there's no limit to how many ships' shields a BM can take down if one of them is in contact. A ship overlapping a ship in contact with a BM also counts as in contact — but this does not cascade further (ship #3 overlapping ship #2 which overlaps ship #1 is not affected unless the BM touches it).
- BMs placed on a target don't affect ships near but not in base contact; place them so they don't touch such bases.
- A ship moving into contact with another ship that has a BM, but not touching the BM itself, does **not** count as in contact with a BM.

### Blast Marker Effects (p. 69)

**Movement**
- A vessel moving through any BMs (including moving away from BMs it starts in contact with) **reduces its speed by 5 cm** that phase, regardless of how many it moves through.
- A ship that cannot slow down (e.g. All Ahead Full) and contacts a BM within the **last 5 cm** of its move must stop at the point of contact.
- A ship with **Shield strength 0** (Eldar, or Shields Collapse critical) moving through any BMs suffers **1 damage on a D6 roll of 6** (once per move, regardless of how many BMs).
- An **Ordnance marker** (torpedo salvo, bomber squadron...) that moves over a BM is **removed on a D6 roll of 6**.

**Shooting**
- If a ship's **line of fire passes through any BMs** (including those in contact with a ship's base), or the firer or target is in base contact with BMs, it suffers **one column shift right** on the Gunnery Table. **Lances and nova cannon are unaffected.**
- BMs take effect immediately, so they affect later shooting in the same turn.

**Shields**
- If a ship's base is in contact with BMs when fired upon, its **shield Strength is reduced by 1 per BM**.
- Ships whose shields are overloaded by fire but are otherwise functional do *not* risk the D6-roll-of-6 damage when moving through BMs.

**Leadership**
- Ships with BMs in base contact suffer −1 Ld to Command checks ("Under Fire", see [01-fundamentals.md](01-fundamentals.md#command-check-modifiers)).

## Shields (p. 70)

- **Each shield blocks one point of damage** in a single Shooting Phase. Place a BM touching the ship's base for each hit blocked.
- Once the number of BMs in contact with the base **equals its number of shields**, the shields absorb nothing further that turn; further hits damage the ship.
- Shields stay down until the ship **moves away from the BMs** in a subsequent Movement Phase.
- Shields only work against **lances, weapons batteries and nova cannon**. **Ramming, bombers and torpedoes** get inside shields — no protection.

*Example:* Iron Duke (2 shields) takes 3 hits from Unclean. 2 absorbed → 2 BMs placed in contact; 3rd hit = 1 damage. Further hits this turn go straight to the hull.

## Catastrophic Damage (p. 71)

- When an **escort** is destroyed, replace it with a BM placed as centrally as possible where the escort was. (A BM is smaller than a small base, so it only takes down a shield if bases actually overlapped.)
- When a **capital ship** is reduced to 0 hits, roll **2D6** on the Catastrophic Damage Table.

### Hulks
- Blazing or drifting hulks **block lines of fire** passing over their base.
- Hulks have no shields, holofields etc.; **turrets 0**. Bombers don't subtract turrets vs hulks.
- Friendly ships/ordnance may not fire on or board a friendly hulk (to deny VPs or trigger catastrophic damage). Torpedoes that hit a friendly hulk still inflict hits, but cannot be deliberately aimed at one.
- Enemies may fire on a hulk. If the hulk is the closest target, they don't need a Ld test to ignore it; firing on a hulk that is not the closest target requires the normal Ld test.
- When shooting at hulks use the destroyed ship's Armour. Roll **once** on the Catastrophic Damage Table each time hits are inflicted by a given ship, squadron or ordnance wave (not once per hit).
- **Exploding ships** count as area effect: holofields can't save, shields work normally.
- Placing BMs from an exploding ship: one BM exactly where the ship was, then the rest fanned around and in contact with it, without overlapping.

### Catastrophic Damage Table (p. 71)

| 2D6 | Blast Markers | Result |
|---:|---|---|
| 2–6 | 1 | **Drifting Hulk**: the ship becomes a shattered hulk. The wreck moves **4D6 cm forward** in each of its subsequent Movement Phases. Place a BM in contact with the wreck's base after each move. |
| 7–8 | 1 | **Blazing Hulk**: a burning wreck. Moves **4D6 cm forward** in each subsequent Movement Phase. Place a BM in contact after its move **and roll on the Catastrophic Damage Table again** after its move. |
| 9–11 | Special | **Plasma Drive Overload**: remove the ship, leaving BMs equal to **half its starting hits**. Every ship within **3D6 cm** is struck by **lance shots with Strength = half the ship's starting damage capacity**. Roll to hit normally. |
| 12 | Special | **Warp Drive Implosion**: remove the ship, leaving BMs equal to **its starting hits**. Every ship within **3D6 cm** is struck by **lance shots with Strength = the wreck's starting damage capacity**. Roll to hit normally. |
