# 11 — Scenarios (pp. 118–143)

## Choosing a Scenario (p. 120)

The rulebook recommends starting with **Cruiser Clash** (Scenario One).

- **Arbitrary**: players just pick one.
- **Random**: roll D6 — 1–3 roll on Raids, 4–6 roll on Battles (or decide raid/battle and roll on that table).

| D6 | Raids | Battles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One: Cruiser Clash | Nine: Exterminatus! |
| 2 | Two: The Bait | Four: Surprise Attack |
| 3 | Three: The Raiders | Seven: Planetary Assault |
| 4 | Five: Blockade Run | Eight: Escalating Engagement |
| 5–6 | Six: Convoy | Ten: Fleet Engagement |

## Attack Ratings (p. 120)

| Fleet | Attack Rating |
|---|---|
| Imperial | 2 |
| Chaos | 2 |
| Tau | 2 |
| Orks | 3 |
| Craftworld Eldar | 3 |
| Space Marines | 3 |
| Tyranids | 3 |
| Corsair Eldar | 4 |
| Dark Eldar | 4 |
| Necrons | 4 |

- **Choosing a scenario**: each player rolls D6 equal to their attack rating and keeps the best single die. Highest has launched the attack and chooses the scenario (and is the attacker). Draw → random scenario.
- **Attacker/defender** (p. 121): decide, randomize, or use attack ratings the same way (highest single die = attacker).

## Scenario Format (p. 122)
Title/Overview, Forces, Battlezone, Set-up, First Turn, Special Rules, Game Length, Victory Conditions.

## Victory Points (pp. 122–123)

| Event | VPs to the opponent |
|---|---|
| Ship destroyed | Its full points value (incl. Admiral/Warmaster/Chaos Lord on board and upgrades) |
| Capital ship crippled | 25% of its points value (round up) |
| Ship/escort squadron disengaged | 25% (round up) if crippled before disengaging; otherwise 10% (round up). An escort squadron is crippled if it lost half its ships (round up). |
| Escorts destroyed | Each escort's value — **only if the entire squadron is destroyed** |
| Planetary defence destroyed | Its points value |
| Holding the field | 50% of the points value of **each hulk (friend or foe)** on the table (round up) |
| Scenario objectives | As per scenario |

- **Holding the field**: all enemy ships destroyed or disengaged, and you have at least one operational ship on the table at the end.

## Pre-Battle Summary (p. 123)

1. Determine scenario.
2. Decide attacker and defender.
3. Choose forces.
4. Set up celestial phenomena ([10-battlefield.md](10-battlefield.md)).
5. **Generate Leadership** for ships and squadrons ([01-fundamentals.md](01-fundamentals.md#starting-leadership-values-one-off-games)).
6. Declare capital ship squadrons; assign Fleet Commanders.
7. Deploy fleets.
8. Determine first turn.
9. Start fighting!

## Scenario One: Cruiser Clash (p. 128) — *introductory scenario*

> Two forces of opposing cruisers run into each other near a system's jump point and immediately attack. The side which inflicts the most damage wins. Intended to be played several times while learning the rules.

**Forces**: Each fleet has **1–4 cruisers** (both sides the **same number**). Each ship worth **no more than 185 points**, chosen from the fleet lists. (The rulebook's own example ships are the Imperial **Lunar** and Chaos **Murder** — see [ships-lunar-murder.md](ships-lunar-murder.md).)

**Battlezone**: For your first game, **no celestial phenomena**.

**Set-up**:
1. Roll **Leadership** for each ship first (p. 45 table).
2. One player rolls a D6: **1–3** → they deploy in **Fleet A** zone; **4–6** → **Fleet B** zone.
3. Both players roll a D6; the **lowest** sets up one cruiser first, then players **alternate** placing ships until all are deployed.
4. Ships may be placed anywhere in their own zone, but **must face towards the opposite long table edge**.

**Deployment map** (p. 128): the table's long edges are top and bottom. **Fleet A**'s zone runs along the top long edge, **Fleet B**'s along the bottom long edge. Each zone is **90 cm wide** (roughly centred along the edge) and the zones are **60 cm apart**. The map doesn't give the table size or zone depth — see the note below.

> **Implementation note (not from the rulebook):** a reasonable digital default is a 180 × 120 cm table (the standard 6'×4'), with each zone 90 cm wide, centred, and 30 cm deep, which leaves the specified 60 cm gap between the zones.

**First turn**: both roll a D6; highest chooses to go first or second.

**Game length**: **8 complete turns each**, or until one fleet has all its ships destroyed.

**Victory conditions** (normal VPs are *not* used):
- **1 point per point of damage** inflicted on enemy ships;
- **+1 point** for each **crippled** enemy ship; or
- **+3 points** for each **destroyed** enemy ship (instead of the +1 for crippled — not both).
- Most points wins.

*Example:* an enemy ship suffers 5 damage → 5 points + 1 for crippled = 6.

### Cruiser Clash — Alternatives (p. 129)
- **Forces**: remove the 185-point cap (allowing nova cannons and launch bays), or allow one carrier each, or play to a points value (**750 points** is a good start; **1,000** to include fleet commanders).
- **Battlezone**: add a few gas/dust clouds, then planets/asteroid fields; eventually use full celestial phenomena: D6 1–3 outer reaches, 4–6 deep space.
- **Set-up**: use the Fleet Engagement set-up (Scenario Ten).
- **Victory**: use standard victory points.

## Scenario Two: The Bait (p. 130)
- **Overview**: a lone ship lures pursuers toward its waiting friends.
- **Forces** (raid, ~750 pts): Pursuers up to 500 pts. Pursued: one ship or squadron up to 250 pts, plus up to 500 pts of reinforcements.
- **Battlezone**: D6 1–3 outer reaches, 4–6 deep space.
- **Set-up**: pursued vessel in the table centre, facing a short edge. Pursuers deployed **more than 60 cm behind it**. Reinforcements enter from the table edge in front of the pursued ship.
- **First turn**: the fleeing ship.
- **Special**: reinforcements may enter on any turn (including turn 1). After turn 1, they may be placed up to **30 cm along the long edges per turn after the first** (e.g. turn 4 → short edge or up to 90 cm along a long edge).
- **Game length**: until one fleet disengages or is destroyed.
- **Victory**: standard VPs.

## Scenario Three: The Raiders (p. 131)
- **Forces**: agree a points limit; defender spends up to it, attacker up to **half**.
- **Battlezone**: mutually agreed.
- **Set-up**: defender deploys all first, all facing the same table edge, ≥ 30 cm from any edge, each ship/squadron ≥ 20 cm apart. Attacker moves on from any edge in his first turn.
- **First turn**: attacker.
- **Special**: for the first **D6 turns**, all defending ships suffer **−1 Leadership**.
- **Game length**: 8 turns or until one fleet disengages.
- **Victory**: standard VPs.

## Scenario Four: Surprise Attack (p. 132)
- **Forces**: equal points. Defender gets an extra **D6×10 pts of planetary defences per 500 pts** (or part) in his fleet.
- **Battlezone**: primary or inner biosphere. Planet in the table centre: ≤500 pts small, 500–1,500 medium, >1,500 large. Then sunward and other phenomena.
- **Set-up**: defender chooses **D3** ships/squadrons on full alert — set up anywhere ≥ 30 cm from an edge. The rest are on **standby**: at least one ship within 15 cm of the planet and all ships abeam of the planet's surface. Attacker moves on from a table edge of choice in his first turn.
- **First turn**: attacker.
- **Special**: standby ships may not move, fire or launch ordnance; they may Brace and repair; turrets/shields work. To go on alert: pass a **Leadership test** (not a Command check — failure doesn't stop other tests). No special orders on the turn a ship goes on alert.
- **Game length**: until one fleet disengages or is destroyed.
- **Victory**: standard VPs.

## Scenario Five: Blockade Run (p. 133)
- **Forces**: agree a total; blockader spends it, attacker up to **half**.
- **Battlezone**: outer reaches or deep space.
- **Set-up**: divide the table **lengthways into thirds**. Blockader rolls a D6 per ship/squadron to determine which third it deploys in (any facing), none within **60 cm of the attacker's table edge**. Attacker sets up within **15 cm** of his table edge.
- **First turn**: roll-off; winner chooses.
- **Game length**: **6 turns**.
- **Victory**: standard VPs, plus the attacker scores the **points value of any ships moved off the blockader's table edge** (crippled ships: a quarter of their value).

## Scenario Six: Convoy (pp. 134–135)
- **Forces**: defender has **≥ 2 transports**; for every two transports, up to **100 pts** of escorting ships (max one squadron per pair). Transports may form one squadron.
- **Attackers** are random: **D3 rolls + 1 per pair of transports** on:

| D6 | Attacker force |
|---|---|
| 1 | One deadfall torpedo or attack craft cluster |
| 2 | Two deadfall torpedo or attack craft clusters |
| 3 | Three deadfall torpedo or attack craft clusters |
| 4 | Escort squadron up to 100 pts |
| 5 | Escort squadron up to 150 pts |
| 6 | One capital ship up to 200 pts |

- **Battlezone**: mutually agreed or rolled.
- **Set-up**: attacker places a face-down **Contact marker** for each capital ship, squadron, deadfall salvo or attack craft cluster: ≥ 30 cm apart, not within 30 cm of an edge (double up if they don't fit). Convoy player rolls for which short edge the convoy enters from (not within 45 cm of either long edge).
- **First turn**: convoy player; ships move on from the entry point. Ships not on by turn 2 are lost in the warp.
- **Special**: a counter activates when a convoy vessel moves **within 30 cm** (flip it; finish the move; deploy after the convoy's Movement Phase). The attacker may voluntarily activate **one** counter at the start of his Movement Phase (**two** if any convoy ships are on special orders).
  - **Deadfall torpedoes**: replace with a **D6+2 Strength** salvo, speed **30 cm**, facing chosen by attacker; moves from the next Ordnance Phase.
  - **Attack craft**: replace with **D3+1** attack craft squadrons (any mix, optionally a wave); move from the next Ordnance Phase.
  - **Squadron**: one ship on the marker, the rest in formation no closer to the enemy; any facing, all the same.
  - **Capital ship**: placed on the marker, any facing.
- **Game length**: until the last transport leaves the table or is destroyed.
- **Victory** — transports exiting via the opposite short edge: **0** Attackers Win; **1** Attackers Marginal Win; **2** Convoy Marginal Win; **3+** Convoy Win.

## Scenario Seven: Planetary Assault (pp. 136–137)
- **Forces**: equal points. Defender: extra **D6×10 pts** of planetary defences per 500 pts. Attacker: **two free transports per 500 pts** (or part).
- **Battlezone**: primary or inner biosphere. Planet ≤ 150 cm from a short edge (D6: 1 small, 2–5 medium, 6 large). Declare sunward; other phenomena as normal.
- **Set-up**: defender places ships/squadrons on **patrol** or on **standby in high orbit** within the gravity well. For each patrol ship/squadron roll D6: 1–3 attacker places it, 4–6 defender does (anywhere not within 30 cm of an edge or inside phenomena; defender always picks facing). Attacker deploys within **15 cm** of the short edge farthest from the planet. A **low orbit table** is needed.
- **First turn**: roll-off.
- **Special**: attacking ships within **30 cm of the planet edge on the low-orbit table** score **assault points** each turn: capital ship **1**, transport **2**. A ship deploying troops/bombarding does nothing else that turn.
- **Game length**: until one fleet is destroyed/disengages or the attacker has **10+ assault points**.
- **Victory**: assault points **+1 per 500 VPs** (round down) the attacker scored, **−1 per 500 VPs** (round up) the defender scored. **0–1** Defender's Victory; **2–5** Defender's Marginal Win; **6–9** Attacker's Marginal Win; **10+** Attacker's Victory.

## Scenario Eight: Escalating Engagement (pp. 138–139)
- **Forces**: each fleet split into **five divisions**, each assigned to a Contact marker (all five must have ships; drawn randomly).
- **Battlezone**: mutually agreed.
- **Set-up**: each player randomly picks one marker as their starting force. Roll for who places first; markers anywhere not within 30 cm of an edge or 60 cm of an enemy marker. Deploy that division within **10 cm** of its marker.
- **First turn**: roll-off.
- **Special**: in each of your End Phases, randomly choose another marker and place it on a randomly rolled table edge: not within 60 cm of enemy ships; if friendly ships are within 30 cm of that edge, within 30 cm of them. At the start of your turn roll a D6 per marker on an edge — arrival needs: slowest ship **up to 20 cm: 5+**; **25 cm: 4+**; **30 cm+: 3+** (**+2** if friendly ships are within 30 cm of the marker). Arrivals enter within 10 cm of the marker. On a failure the marker may be moved along the edge up to the division's slowest speed.
- **Game length**: until one fleet disengages or is destroyed.
- **Victory**: standard VPs.

## Scenario Nine: Exterminatus (pp. 140–141)
- **Forces**: agreed total. Attacker may convert **one capital ship per 1,000 pts** (or part) into an **Exterminator**: its prow armament is replaced by an **Armageddon weapon** (only usable vs planets). A Chaos attacker may use an active Blackstone Fortress instead (no weapons lost). Defender gets extra **D6×10 pts** of planetary defences per 500 pts.
- **Battlezone**: primary or inner biosphere; planet ≤ 150 cm from a short edge (D6: 1 small, 2–5 medium, 6 large); sunward declared.
- **Set-up**: defender keeps **one capital ship or escort squadron per 500 pts on patrol** (off-table); the rest deploy anywhere not within 30 cm of an edge. Attacker sets up within 15 cm of the edge farthest from the planet. Low orbit table needed.
- **First turn**: roll-off.
- **Special**: Exterminators must enter low orbit and get within **45 cm of the planet edge**. At the start of each turn an Exterminator is there, roll D6: **4+** destroys all life on the planet. Defenders may always target an Exterminator without a Ld test. Patrols arrive at the start of each defender turn on a D6 (speed ≤ 20 cm: 5+; 25 cm: 4+; 30 cm+: 3+) on a random table edge.
- **Campaign**: if the planet is destroyed: D6 1–3 system uninhabited; 4–6 primary world destroyed, roll again: 1–3 agri-world, 4–6 mining planet.
- **Game length**: until one fleet disengages, all Exterminators are destroyed, or the planet is destroyed.
- **Victory**: a fleet that disengages loses; all Exterminators destroyed → defender wins; planet destroyed → attacker wins.

## Scenario Ten: Fleet Engagement (pp. 142–143)
- **Forces**: equal points.
- **Battlezone**: mutually agreed.
- **Set-up**: each player secretly chooses a formation — **Sphere** (envelops; vulnerable to wedge), **Wedge** (packed; breaks thin lines; easily surrounded), **Cross** (parallel broadside line). Cross-reference:

| Your choice ↓ / Opponent's → | Sphere | Wedge | Cross |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sphere** | B | A(d.grey)/C(d.grey) | A(d.grey)/D(d.grey) |
| **Wedge** | A(white)/C(white) | D(d.grey)/D(white) | B |
| **Cross** | A(white)/D(white) | B | B |

  The label in brackets says which zone colour on the map **you** use. On a split result (e.g. A/D), both roll a D6: **+1** if your fastest ship is faster than any enemy ship, **+1** for the fleet with the best Admiral (highest Ld), **+1** for the fleet with the most escorts. The winner picks the set-up.
- Then both roll a D6; lowest deploys a squadron/lone ship first; alternate until done.
- **Divisions**: if a zone is split into divisions, deploy at least one ship/squadron in each.
- **Approach angle**: ships must face along the arrows of their division.

**Set-up maps** (p. 143; transcribed from the diagrams; long edges top/bottom):
- **A**: *White*: a block along the top edge, 60 cm wide, set 30 cm in from the side zones, facing down (toward bottom). *Dark grey*: three divisions — a strip along each short edge (heading diagonally inward toward the top), and a 30 cm-deep strip along the bottom edge between them, facing up. 60 cm between the white block and the bottom strip.
- **B**: *White*: three divisions along the top edge (centre division 45 cm wide), all heading **right**. *Dark grey*: three divisions along the bottom edge (centre 45 cm wide), all heading **left**. 60 cm gap between.
- **C**: *White*: a block 45 cm deep at top centre, 30 cm in from the side strips, facing down. *Dark grey*: two 30 cm-wide strips along the short edges, heading up (toward the top edge).
- **D**: *White*: a 30 cm-deep strip along the top edge, 45 cm in from each short edge, facing down. *Dark grey*: three divisions along the bottom edge (centre 60 cm wide), facing up. 60 cm gap between.
- **First turn**: roll-off. **Game length**: until one fleet disengages or is destroyed. **Victory**: standard VPs.

## Sub-Plots (pp. 124–127)

Each player may generate a sub-plot (mandatory in campaigns). Roll D6: **1–2** Legacies of War; **3–4** Chance Circumstances; **5–6** Secret Ploys.

**Battle results**:

| Objectives | Result |
|---|---|
| Completed main mission and sub-plot | **Heroic Victory!** |
| Completed main mission | **Victory!** |
| Failed main mission but completed sub-plot | **Heroic Defeat!** |
| Failed main mission and sub-plot | **Miserable Defeat!** |

**Renown** (campaigns): shown as (gained on completion / lost on failure).

### Legacies of War (1–2)

| D6 | Sub-plot | Effect | Objective (renown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gunnery Practice | D6 per capital ship: on 4+ it gets **+1 Ld for Lock On**. | Inflict ≥ 1 damage on every enemy capital ship. (+1/−1) |
| 2 | Extended Duty | D6 per capital ship: **1** = starts with D3 less damage points; **2** = −1 Ld for Lock On & Reload Ordnance; **3+** unaffected. | Destroy/cripple more points than you lose. (+1/0) |
| 3 | Blood Bond | If one of your capital ships is crippled or destroyed, all your other capital ships gain **+1 Ld** next turn. | Finish with less than half your capital ships crippled/destroyed. (+1/−1) |
| 4 | Fleet Experience | Any ship within 30 cm of your fleet commander's ship may use his Ld. | Commander's ship must survive with ≥ 75% of its original damage. (+1/0) |
| 5 | No Quarter! | — | Cripple/destroy ≥ half the enemy fleet's points value. (+1/−1) |
| 6 | Vendetta | — | Nominate an enemy capital ship; cripple or destroy it. (+1/−2) |

### Chance Circumstances (3–4)

| D6 | Sub-plot | Effect | Objective (renown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meteor Storm! | D6 per capital ship: on 1–3 roll once on the Critical Damage table and apply at game start. | None. (+3/−2) |
| 2 | Signalling Problems | Fleet commander re-rolls only for squadrons with a ship within 30 cm of the commander's ship. | ≥ half your escorts survive. (+1/−1) |
| 3 | Unexpected Help | Add one capital ship or escort squadron worth D3×50 pts. | At least one reinforcement ship survives uncrippled. (0/−2) |
| 4 | Navigational Hazards | D6: 1–2 one extra gas/dust cloud; 3–4 two extra clouds; 5 an extra asteroid field; 6 an extra asteroid field and D3 clouds. | None. (+1/−1) |
| 5 | Fresh Captain | A random capital ship (not the commander's) gets **−1 Ld**. | That ship must cripple/destroy enemy ships worth ≥ its own points value. (+1/−1) |
| 6 | Enemy Plans | Each boarding action vs an enemy capital ship: D6 4+ finds the plans (+1 vs enemy commander's ship). | Capture the enemy's battle plans. (+1/−1) |

### Secret Ploys (5–6)

| D6 | Sub-plot | Effect | Objective (renown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surprise Attack! | Enemy fleet cannot go on special orders for the first D3 turns. | None. (+2/−2) |
| 2 | Secret Intelligence | Gain an extra fleet commander re-roll. | Nominate an enemy capital ship; board it or Hit-and-Run it at least once. (0/−1) |
| 3 | Experimental Ship | Random capital ship: roll D6 on each table below (re-roll the side effect if it contradicts). | Prevent it from being crippled or destroyed. (+1/−1) |
| 4 | Desperate Mission | — | Nominate one of your capital ships; prevent it being crippled or destroyed. (+1/−1) |
| 5 | Decoy | Add an extra "capital ship" that is really a transport (secret until it fires or is fired on). Worth 0 VP. | Succeed if an enemy capital ship fires at the decoy. (+1/−1) |
| 6 | Hit-and-Run | Roll 2D6 = turns you have to win. | Win within that many turns. (+1/−1) |

**Experimental Ship tables**:

| D6 | Improvement | Side effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | All weapons batteries/lances +50% range | −1 shield |
| 2 | +1 shield | All weapons batteries/lances at 50% range |
| 3 | Speed +5 cm | −2 damage |
| 4 | +2 damage | Speed −5 cm |
| 5 | Ship may make 90° turns | −1 Leadership |
| 6 | +1 Leadership | Weapon battery firepower/lances at 50% strength (round up) |
