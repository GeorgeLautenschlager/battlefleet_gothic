# 07 — The End Phase (pp. 88–92)

The End Phase is performed **in this order**:

1. Conduct **Boarding Actions / Hit-and-Run attacks**.
2. Attempt to **repair critical damage** (both players).
3. **Remove D6 Blast Markers** (player whose turn it is).

## Damage Control (p. 88)

- **Both players** may attempt repairs.
- Capital ships roll **1D6 per Damage point remaining**. Each **6** repairs one critically damaged system or puts out one fire.
- If the ship has **any Blast Markers in contact**, roll only **half as many dice** (rounding up).
- Unrepaired **Fire!** criticals each cause 1 damage and keep burning (see [05-damage.md](05-damage.md#critical-hits-table-p-67)).
- Some criticals can't be repaired (Bridge Smashed, Shields Collapse).

## Blast Marker Removal (p. 88)

- The player whose turn it is rolls a **D6** and removes that many Blast Markers.
- **Blast Markers in contact with ships' bases may not be removed**; otherwise the player chooses which.

## Boarding Actions (pp. 89–91)

- A player may **never initiate** a ship-to-ship boarding action during the enemy's turn (but defends normally).
- **Declaring**: a ship may board if it is in **base contact** with an enemy vessel in the End Phase (before damage control / BM removal). The boarding must be **declared in the Movement Phase** when contact is made.
- Ships attempting to board **may not fire weapons or launch ordnance that turn**.

### Boarding Value (p. 89)
- = the number of **damage points remaining**, modified by special rules (e.g. Mark of Khorne doubles it; Tau Kor'vattra halves it).
- The **defending** ship then **adds its remaining turrets**.
- This final value determines the boarding-value modifiers below.

### Resolving (p. 90)
Both players roll **D6 + modifiers**:

| Condition | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Own boarding value higher* | +1 |
| Own boarding value is twice the enemy's* | +2 |
| Own boarding value is three times the enemy's* | +3 |
| Own boarding value is four or more times the enemy's* | +4 |
| Enemy ship has Blast Markers in direct base contact | +1 |
| Enemy ship is crippled | +2 |
| Enemy ship is on special orders | +1 |
| Orks or Chaos | +1 |
| Space Marines | +2 |

\* Only apply the highest of these.

- **Highest total wins.** The loser suffers **1 damage per point it lost by**. Totals are not capped at 6.
- Critical chance for each ship:

| Difference | Result | Critical hit on (Winner / Loser) |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Stalemate | 5+ / 5+ |
| 2 | Heavy Fighting | 4+ / 5+ |
| 3 | Driven Back | 3+ / 6+ |
| 4 | Stormed | 2+ / 6+ |
| 5+ | Overwhelmed | Auto / None |

  *Note: the column header reads "Winners/Losers", but the worked example implies the first value applies to the **loser**: Hyperion (loser by 2, Heavy Fighting) suffers a critical on 4+, Plagueclaw (winner) on 5+. So read it as **Loser / Winner**.*

- A ship reduced to 0 damage by boarding **does not roll on the Catastrophic Damage table** — it automatically becomes a **drifting hulk** (exception: if reduced to 0 by a critical hit, roll catastrophic damage normally).
- Ships surviving boarding may move off normally next Movement Phase.
- Brace For Impact! does **not** protect against boarding damage or boarding criticals.

*Example:* Hyperion (3 damage left) boarded by Plagueclaw (6 left). After modifiers Plagueclaw scores 6, Hyperion 4. Hyperion loses by 2 → takes 2 damage; Heavy Fighting → Hyperion suffers a critical on 4+, Plagueclaw on 5+.

### Drawn Combats
- Equal totals = draw. The ships grapple and **continue fighting in each subsequent End Phase**. Neither may move, shoot or launch ordnance until one is reduced to zero damage.

### Multi-Ship Boarding
- Attacker chooses **together** or **separately**.
- **Together**: add boarding values; one roll. Race/other modifiers counted once (highest applies). Attacker distributes damage among attackers; critical chance applies to every boarding ship.
- **Separately**: each capital ship / escort squadron rolls separately vs the defender (damage to defender is cumulative); each uses only its own boarding value.

### Escorts Boarding
- Escorts can board (even capital ships, though they only stand a chance against badly damaged ones). A critical hit destroys an escort, so an escort can win a boarding action and be destroyed.

## Hit-and-Run Attacks (pp. 91–92)

- Made by **assault boats**, **boarding torpedoes**, or **teleport attacks**.
- Ships that can't be boarded (e.g. Mark of Nurgle) are **not** automatically immune.

### Assault Boats
- Launched from flight decks, move **30 cm** any direction each Ordnance Phase. On contact, shot at by turrets like bombers; each survivor makes **one Hit-and-Run attack** in the Ordnance Phase, then is removed.

### Boarding Torpedoes
- Attack like torpedoes (turrets, to-hit); each hit makes a Hit-and-Run attack instead of 1 damage.

### Teleport Attacks
- In the **End Phase**, against an enemy ship **with no shields** (shields knocked down) within **10 cm**.
- Rulebook text: *"Teleport attacks can be conducted by capital ships that are on Lock On or Reload Ordnance special orders. All other special orders preclude the use of teleport attacks."* (Interpretation: capital ships on no special orders, Lock On, or Reload Ordnance may teleport; ships on any other special order may not.)
- **Crippled ships cannot** teleport. Escorts/defences with 1 damage point left cannot. A ship can't teleport-attack a target that currently has **more hits remaining than itself**.
- One teleport attack per ship per turn = one Hit-and-Run attack.

### Resolving a Hit-and-Run Attack
- Roll a **D6**: on a **1** it fails; on **2+**, look up the score **on the Critical Hits Table** (as if it were the 2D6 result) and apply immediately. Can be repaired normally.
- Fleets with **+1 to Hit-and-Run** effectively roll 2–7 and never fail.
- **Vs escorts**: Hit-and-Run destroys an escort on **4+** (3+ with a +1 bonus; 5+ vs fleets with −1 to enemy H&R). Escorts can Brace For Impact! against it.
- Brace For Impact! **does** protect against Hit-and-Run critical damage.
