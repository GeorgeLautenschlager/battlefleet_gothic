# 09 — Planetary Defences (pp. 100–101)

Used in certain scenarios, usually by a defender protecting a planet. Includes armed satellites, ground installations, system ships (no warp drive), minefields, etc.

## Unit types

- **Satellite** units **may not move** (they're in orbit or in deep space). Their weapons fire **all round** — no fire arcs.
- **Ground** units can't move (in underground silos). Limited to a **single 90° fire arc** extending up from the planet's surface.

## Special Rules (p. 100)

- Orbital defences (or ships counting as defences) may form squadrons: **1-HP defences in groups of up to six**, larger defences up to four. A Ramilies Star Fort can't squadron with other orbital defences.
- Stationary defences may be in base contact but never stacked/overlapping.
- The defender may spend **up to one-third** of their points on planetary defences (on top of any the scenario provides), drawn from their fleet points. For large fleet bases, use the Imperial Blackstone Fortress profile/cost.
- Stationary defences and defence-ships (Defence Monitors, system ships) **do not roll for Leadership**; they reload ordnance on a nominal **Ld 7** (unless stated otherwise). They can't take any special orders except **Reload Ordnance**, but may attempt **Brace For Impact!** on Ld 7. All their other Ld checks (navigating phenomena, ignoring closest target…) are against **Ld 7**.
- Ships merely *targeted* as defences (Ork Roks, Kroot Warspheres…) are treated as ships and use special orders normally.
- When shooting at minefields, treat them as ordnance for target priority.

## Defences Critical Hits Table (p. 101)

If a critical can't be applied (e.g. no lances), apply the next highest. Multiple criticals in the same place must all be repaired.

| 2D6 | Extra damage | Result |
|---:|---|---|
| 2–3 | +0 | **Lances Damaged**: station's lances may not fire until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Main Armament Damaged**: weapons batteries may not fire until repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Ordnance Bays Hit**: no ordnance may be fired until repaired. |
| 6 | +1 | **Reactors Damaged**: shields and turrets at half Strength until repaired. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!**: roll to extinguish in the End Phase; if not put out, 1 extra damage and keeps burning. |
| 8–9 | +1 | **Orbit Lost**: the defence falls **D6 cm** towards the planet each of its Movement Phases until repaired or it hits the planet and is destroyed. |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields Collapse**: shields reduced to zero; cannot be repaired. |
| 11 | +D3 | **Hull Breach**. |
| 12 | +D6 | **Bulkhead Collapse**. |

## Defences with Multiple Hits (p. 101)
- Large platforms (space stations, Blackstone Fortresses) take damage like capital ships: critical hits, crippled at half damage, Catastrophic Damage table at 0 — but **defences never drift**; they stay in place.

## Warp Rifts
- Planetary defences may not be placed within **30 cm** of a warp rift.

## Blast Marker Removal
- In each End Phase, after all other End Phase actions, remove **D6 Blast Markers from each defence with speed 0 cm** (stationary defences only — not monitors/system ships, ships on standby, or ships temporarily reduced to 0 cm).
