# 14 — Andy Chambers' Notes (pp. 200–201)

Designer clarifications and intent from the original game designer. Several are useful rulings.

## The most important rule
If a situation isn't covered by the rules, **roll a D6** and let fate decide — keep the game moving. Changes are fine if your opponent agrees.

## Leadership
- **Random Leadership** is intentional. The designer rejects "best crews on biggest ships" and crew swapping; commanders and squadrons mitigate bad Leadership. Want control? Play a campaign.

## The Turn
- The alternating turn sequence is intentionally simple. Variants he considered (for house rules): alternating ship-by-ship activation, speed-based initiative, random activation counters, "A moves / B shoots".

## Movement Phase
- **No accidental collisions** — deliberate; only intentional rams cause contact damage.

## Shooting Phase
- **On the line**: if a target sits exactly on the line between two fire arcs, the player chooses which arc it's in — but **not both**.
- **Halted ships**: a ship brought to a halt (criticals + Blast Markers) or holding station in a gravity well is targeted on the **Defences** column.
- **Blast Markers take effect immediately**, even on later shooting by the *same* ship in the same Shooting Phase. Hint: **fire weapons batteries before lances** (lances ignore BM column shifts; the BMs from lance hits would shift your batteries).
- A ship moving away from BMs in contact with its base **always suffers the 5 cm speed penalty**, even moving directly away. Being slowed by BMs does **not** reduce the distance a capital ship must move before turning, nor the half-speed minimum distance.

## Ordnance Phase
- **Ordnance moves in both players' turns.** Attack craft may choose not to move in an Ordnance Phase; **torpedoes must move full speed** each Ordnance Phase.
- **Varying armour**: **bombers** always roll against the target's **lowest** armour. **Torpedoes** attack the armour of the **facing they strike first**.

## End Phase
- **Shields** are knocked down/"regenerated" purely by Blast Markers — there is no repair roll for shields. Keep moving out of the BMs and they come back automatically.
