# Ship Profiles: Lunar & Murder (p. 43)

These are the rulebook's own worked-example ships, used throughout the core rules' examples (the *Agrippa* and the *Unclean*). They're the only full ship profiles in this rulebook. Their profiles match the Fleets book entries ([Lunar](fleets/imperial-navy/vessels.md#lunar-class-cruiser--180-pts--p-71), [Murder](fleets/chaos/vessels.md#murder-class-cruiser--170-pts--p-279)), which add **points costs** and **options**:

| | Lunar (Fleets p. 71) | Murder (Fleets p. 279) |
|---|---|---|
| Points | **180 pts** | **170 pts** |
| Option | Replace prow torpedoes with a **nova cannon** for +20 pts | Replace the FP 10 batteries with FP 4 batteries + Str 2 lance batteries (all 45 cm, port/starboard), no extra cost; max two per 750 pts |

Both are under Cruiser Clash's 185-point cap, so the standard Lunar vs Murder is a legal Cruiser Clash pairing.

## Imperial Lunar class cruiser — "Agrippa"

| Type/Hits | Speed | Turns | Shields | Armour | Turrets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cruiser/8 | 20 cm | 45° | 2 | 6+ front / 5+ | 2 |

| Armament | Range/Speed | Firepower/Str | Fire Arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port lance battery | 30 cm | 2 | Left |
| Starboard lance battery | 30 cm | 2 | Right |
| Port weapons battery | 30 cm | 6 | Left |
| Starboard weapons battery | 30 cm | 6 | Right |
| Prow torpedoes | 30 cm (speed) | 6 | Front |

- Leadership shown as 7 in the example. **In a one-off game, roll it** (D6: 1 → 6, 2–3 → 7, 4–5 → 8, 6 → 9).
- **Armour 6+ front / 5+**: armour is 6+ against shots from its **front** arc and 5+ from any other arc. Bombers always use the lowest armour (5+). Torpedoes use the armour of the facing they strike first. Rammers attacking the Lunar head-on use its 6+ front armour (p. 56 example).
- Small base (32 mm): it has 2 shields and 8 hits, so a large base isn't required.

## Worked examples from the rulebook using these ships

- **Command check** (p. 48): Agrippa (Ld 7) tries All Ahead Full; Unclean was on Lock On, so +1 → Ld 8; rolls 2+6 = 8, passes.
- **Ram tests** (p. 55): a Lunar rams a cruiser on 2D6, an escort on 3D6, a battleship on 1D6.
- **Ram** (p. 56): Agrippa rams Unclean's starboard: 8D6 vs 5+ → 3 damage. Unclean replies 4D6 vs Agrippa's front 6+ → 1 damage.
- **Range** (p. 59): Unclean at 34 cm → its batteries (45 cm) are in range.
- **Gunnery** (pp. 60, 62): Unclean's starboard battery (FP 10) at a closing capital ship → **7D6**, needing 6s vs Agrippa's front armour. Within 15 cm → 9D6; beyond 30 cm → 5D6.
- **Lances** (p. 60): Agrippa's Str 2 lances → 2D6, each 4+ hits.
- **Crippling** (p. 65): a Lunar is crippled after 4 damage.
- **Bombers** (p. 83): 2 bomber squadrons vs a Murder (2 turrets): 2 turret dice, survivors make D6−2 attacks each.
