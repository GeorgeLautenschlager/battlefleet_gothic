# 08 — Squadrons (pp. 94–99)

Escorts are normally grouped into squadrons; capital ships may also be squadroned for big battles.

## Squadron Command Checks (p. 95)

- A **single Leadership test** puts the whole squadron on special orders.
- Capital ship squadrons with different Ld values use the **highest surviving Ld** for Command checks. Escort squadrons share a single Ld value.
- Normal Command check modifiers (Enemy Contacts +1, Under Fire −1) apply, even if only some ships have Blast Markers in contact.
- **All ships in a squadron are equally affected** by special orders taken by any one ship — including **Brace For Impact!** (all ships brace, not just the one under fire; replaces current orders). This includes capital ship squadrons.
- All Ahead Full: one 4D6 roll for the whole squadron. Squadrons can only undergo one special order per turn.

## Squadron Composition (p. 95)

- Capital ship squadrons must be **declared before the game**. Escort squadrons are chosen from the fleet list.
- Unless the fleet list says otherwise, an escort squadron can contain **up to six ships**.
- Ships in a squadron may be of different classes but must be of the **same type**.

## Squadron Formation (p. 96)

- Each ship must be **no more than 15 cm** from another ship in the squadron, forming a continuous chain with links of up to 15 cm.
- A ship out of formation no longer counts as part of the squadron until it moves back within 15 cm; it must move back ASAP and may not use the squadron's special orders until it does.
- Ships manoeuvre individually within normal movement rules, as long as they stay in formation.
- If any ship in an escort squadron moves off the table edge, the entire squadron must immediately (and in subsequent turns) only attempt to disengage.

## Shooting at Squadrons (pp. 96–98)

- **Mixed aspects**: the attacker chooses which aspect category to fire at, but **cannot score hits on anything harder to target** than the chosen category. (Two closing + two abeam; fire as closing → only the two closing ships can be hit.)
- The only way to hit a more distant member: with gunnery, if it has more favourable (left) gunnery modifiers, or weaker armour (only hits that couldn't hurt the closest may hit it — armour-ignoring weapons can't exploit this).
- **Allocating hits**: hits go to the **nearest eligible ship** until destroyed, then the next nearest, etc. (shooting only — not ordnance, boarding etc.).
- A Ld test **cannot** be used to pick out individual ships within a squadron (except with ordnance).
- Hits are only distributed among vessels that **actually took fire** (in range and arc). E.g. 10 hits but only 3 escorts in range/arc → at most 3 destroyed. Same for asteroid field hits, ordnance and nova cannon (only ships actually contacted).
- *Example:* Unclean scores 3 hits on a frigate squadron: hit 1 downs the nearest in-arc frigate's shield, hit 2 destroys it, hit 3 downs the next frigate's shield. (A closer frigate out of the Unclean's arc can't be hit.)

### Different Armour values (p. 98)
- Find the **lowest armour** in the squadron (accounting for facings). Any die ≥ the lowest armour scores a hit. Allocate dice one at a time, **lowest rolls first against the nearest targets they can affect**, working upward.
- *Example:* rolls 4, 5, 6 vs a Sword (5+) and Cobra (4+), Sword closer: the 4 goes to the Cobra; 5 and 6 to the Sword.

## Capital Ship Squadrons (p. 98)

- When a capital ship in a squadron is being destroyed by fire, **roll for and apply all its critical damage before it counts as destroyed** (critical extra damage counts against it), then remaining hits pass to the next ship. Roll criticals one hit at a time.
- If a capital squadron disengages entirely, each capital ship calculates its VP percentage separately.

## Shooting by Squadrons (p. 99)

- Squadrons combine their firing.
- **Escort squadrons**: total firepower added up and then **halved (round up)** when on special orders that affect firepower. Escort squadrons are **not** halved merely for being crippled (reduced to half their starting number).
- **Capital ships** in squadrons halve individually (round up).
- A squadron shooting follows single-ship rules: Ld test to fire at anything but the closest enemy. Ships out of range/arc of the primary target may fire at another enemy.
- **Weapons batteries**: add firepower together before consulting the Gunnery Table. If ships fire from different aspects/ranges, calculate per group: e.g. 5 Swords firing at a cruiser, 2 in its rear arc (Moving Away) and 3 on its beam (Abeam) — compute each group's dice separately and add them.
- **Launching ordnance**: ships launch individually unless in **base-to-base contact**, in which case they may combine into a bigger wave/salvo. A combined salvo must be within **all** firing ships' arcs, with its furthest edge at its speed limit from the **furthest** ship's stem. (Some ships, e.g. Ork Ravagers, can't combine.)
